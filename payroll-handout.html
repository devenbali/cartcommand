How Your Pay Works

The more carts we build together, the more every one of us earns. Your hours determine your share. Here's exactly how it breaks down.

Weekly Pay System
Pay period: Monday – Sunday Base pay: $550 @ 40h, prorated if under Overtime: grows your bonus share Weekends: count toward bonus share
The 3 Rules
1
Base Pay — Always Guaranteed

Work 40 hours and you get your full $550 base. Work less and it's prorated. Your base is always paid first, no matter what.

2
The Bonus Pool — Team Effort

Every cart the team builds generates money. After base pay is covered, the rest becomes the bonus pool — shared by everyone in the system.

3
Your Hours = Your Share

The bonus pool splits based on hours worked. More hours means a bigger percentage. Overtime and weekend hours count — no cap on your share.
